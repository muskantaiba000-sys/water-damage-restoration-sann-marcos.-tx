When To Call

Signs you need water damage restoration San Marcos TX

Recognizing the signs of water damage early can save you from extensive repairs. Here are some indicators that you may need water damage restoration San Marcos TX in Buda TX.

1. Water stains on walls or ceilings

Visible water stains indicate that moisture is present, which can lead to mold growth and structural damage if not addressed promptly.

2. Musty odors in your home

A musty smell is often a sign of mold or mildew, which thrives in damp environments. This can pose health risks to your family.

3. Peeling or bubbling paint

If you notice paint peeling or bubbling, it could be a sign of trapped moisture behind the surface, indicating potential water damage.

4. Increased utility bills

Higher water bills may indicate a hidden leak or water intrusion, which can lead to significant damage if not addressed quickly.

5. Warped or buckling floors

Floors that are warped or buckled are often a result of excess moisture. This can compromise the integrity of your flooring materials.
